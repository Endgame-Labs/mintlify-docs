Digests allow users to send custom prompt outputs directly to their email inbox on a daily, weekly, monthly, or quarterly schedule.

## Create or manage existing digests

To create or manage your existing digests, go to **Manage**, then **Digests** in the [user menu](https://app.endgame.io/manage/digests).

Once in the digest view, you can click New Digest to create a digest, or click on an existing digest to make updates. Endgame provides a few suggested digests to get you started. You can enable and edit these digests or create new digests from scratch.

Add additional recipients to your digest by selecting users in your organization
or adding any valid email address to the Additional Email Recipients field.

You can disable or delete digests at any time if you want to stop receiving fresh content. By default, digests are sent to the email address associated with your user.

You can generate a digest preview by clicking Run Now. The preview is sent directly to your email, so you don't have to wait for your first scheduled digest.
