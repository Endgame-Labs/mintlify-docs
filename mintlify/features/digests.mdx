Digests allow users to send custom prompt outputs directly to their email inbox on a daily, weekly, monthly, or quarterly schedule.

## Create or manage existing digests

To create or manage your existing digests, go to [Settings \> You \> Digests](https://app.endgame.io/settings/digests).

To create a digest, click **New digest** at the top right of the page. Endgame provides a few suggested digests to get you started. You can turn on and edit these digests or create new digests from scratch.

Use the search bar, the **All**, **Active**, and **Inactive** filters, or the **Sort** dropdown to find a digest. Each digest card shows its schedule, its recipients, and when it last ran and will run next.

Add additional recipients to your digest by selecting users in your organization
or adding any valid email address to the **Additional recipients** field.

By default, digests are sent to the email address associated with your user.

## Manage a digest

- **Turn a digest on or off:** click the toggle on its card. A blue toggle means the digest is active. When it's off, the card shows **Paused** instead of the next run date. Turn a digest off whenever you want to stop receiving it without losing its settings.
- **Edit a digest:** click anywhere on its card to open it, or select **Edit** from the card's **⋮** menu.
- **Run a digest now:** select **Run digest** from the card's **⋮** menu, or open the digest and click **Run now**. Endgame sends the results straight to your email, so you don't have to wait for the next scheduled run.
- **Delete a digest:** select **Delete** from the card's **⋮** menu.
