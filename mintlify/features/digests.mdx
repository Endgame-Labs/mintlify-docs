A digest is a prompt that Endgame runs on a schedule and emails to you. Use one for anything you'd otherwise ask Endgame again and again, like a weekly pipeline summary or prep for the week's customer calls.

Endgame includes a few suggested digests to get you started. Turn one on as it is, edit it to fit how you work, or create your own.

## Find your digests

Your digests are in [**Settings** > **You** > **Digests**](https://app.endgame.io/settings/digests). Each card shows the digest's schedule, its recipients, and when it last ran and will run next. Use the search bar, the **All**, **Active**, and **Inactive** filters, or **Sort** to find one.

## Create a digest

1. In **Settings** > **You** > **Digests**, click **New digest** at the top right.
2. In **Name**, give the digest a short name you'll recognize in your list, like "Weekly pipeline summary".
3. In **Prompt**, write what you want Endgame to do, the same way you'd ask in chat. Say what to cover, which time period to look at, and how you want the results laid out. For example:

   ```text
   Summarize what changed in my open opportunities over the past week:
   new risks, stage changes, and upcoming close dates.
   Group by account and keep it to a short list per account.
   ```

4. Optional: in **Additional recipients**, add teammates who should also get the digest. Recipients must be active members of your organization. Digests go to you by default.
5. In **Digest schedule**, choose how often it runs (daily, weekdays, weekly, monthly, or quarterly) and at what time. It runs in the timezone shown under the schedule.
6. Click **Create**.

## Test a digest

You don't have to wait for the schedule to see what a digest sends. Run it now, check the email, and adjust the prompt until the results are what you want.

- On the digest's card, open the **⋮** menu and select **Run digest**.
- Or open the digest and click **Run now**.

Endgame emails you the results when the run finishes. The digest's schedule stays the same.

## Manage a digest

- **Turn a digest on or off:** click the toggle on its card. A blue toggle means the digest is active. When it's off, the card shows **Paused** instead of the next run date, and the digest keeps its settings until you turn it back on.
- **Edit a digest:** click anywhere on its card, or select **Edit** from the card's **⋮** menu.
- **Delete a digest:** select **Delete** from the card's **⋮** menu.
