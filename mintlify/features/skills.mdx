## What are skills

A skill is a reusable set of instructions that tells Endgame how to handle a specific type of task.

Think of skills as your organization's playbook. Instead of re-explaining a process every time, you can request a skill and Endgame follows its instructions.

A skill can define:

- The goal of the task (e.g., "write a follow-up email after a discovery call")
- The format or structure the output should follow
- Your company's tone, messaging, or value props to always include
- Step-by-step instructions the AI should walk through
- Examples of emails, call scripts, or summaries that have worked well

For example, your team might always follow up after a demo the same way: thank the customer for their time, recap the top pain points they mentioned, and end with a clear next step. A demo follow-up skill can include those instructions so everyone on the team gets a consistent, on-brand follow-up.

**Why use skills?**

- Save time by avoiding repetitive prompts
- Get consistent, on-brand output across your whole team
- Give users access to proven organizational playbooks

<Note>
  Only account admins can create and manage skills. Admins can learn more in the
  [Admin Guide](https://docs.endgame.io/skills/skills-101).
</Note>

## View available skills

To view the skills available to you:

1. Open the user menu.
2. Select **Manage**.
3. Select **Skills**.

## Use skills in chats

Ask Endgame to use an organization skill in your prompt. Refer to the skill by name and include any context needed for the task.

For example:

> Use the Demo Follow-Up skill to write a follow-up email for the demo I just completed for Acme Co.

Add details or constraints to your prompt as needed. Endgame uses the skill's instructions when generating its response.
