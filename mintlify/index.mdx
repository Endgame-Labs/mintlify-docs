Endgame is transforming enterprise sales by equipping every seller with what we believe is the strongest and most durable competitive advantage: **knowledge**.

## How it works

By using AI to integrate your GTM strategy with first and third-party data sources (emails, calls, CRM, LinkedIn, news, and financial reports) and making all of the information immediately accessible and relevant through our MCP server and our web application.

The result is a dynamic, intelligent context graph: an actionable source of truth for every account and person in your book of business, instantly at your fingertips.
