For enterprise revenue teams putting AI to work, Endgame is the context layer underneath the GTM agents you connect.

## How it works

Endgame connects your CRM, calls, email, Slack, docs and warehouse, turns what was said and written into source-cited facts, and adds relevant public information that its context agents research. Use it in the Endgame web app, connect Claude, ChatGPT or your own agents over MCP, or script it from the terminal with the Endgame CLI.

The result is a context graph: a map of every account, person and opportunity in your book and the relationships between them. Because the context is compiled ahead of time, an agent starts from cited facts that are ready when it asks, rather than digging through raw data, and the web app, your connected agents and the CLI all read from the same graph.
