Connect Confluence so Endgame can use the pages in your Confluence spaces as knowledge. You choose which spaces to sync, and Endgame links their content to accounts where it applies.

## Before you start

- You need to be an Endgame admin.
- Your organization connects Confluence through one Confluence user. Whoever connects should have access to every space you want to sync. Endgame can see all the spaces that person can access, but only syncs the ones you add.

## Connect Confluence

1. In [**Settings** > **Organization** > **Integrations**](https://app.endgame.io/settings/integrations), find Confluence and click **Connect**. You can search for "Confluence", or choose **Available** to show only integrations you haven't connected yet.
2. Sign in to Confluence and approve access.

You'll come back to the Confluence page in Endgame, which shows **Connected** at the top, so you can choose which spaces to sync.

## Choose spaces to sync

- **Add a space:** in **Add a space**, select a space and click **Add**. Everything in a space you add syncs to Endgame.
- **Remove a space:** in **Linked spaces**, open the ⋮ menu next to the space and click **Remove space**. Endgame stops syncing it.

## Find Confluence content in Knowledge

Synced pages appear in [**Settings** > **Organization** > **Knowledge**](https://app.endgame.io/settings/knowledge). Each space is listed as a folder, with its pages inside. To change which spaces sync, use the Confluence page, not Knowledge.

## Update credentials or disconnect

Use the **Connection** section at the bottom of the Confluence page. See [Manage a connected integration](/integrations/overview#manage-a-connected-integration).

- **Update credentials** takes you to Confluence's sign-in page to renew Endgame's access.
- **Disconnect** stops syncing your linked spaces from Confluence.
