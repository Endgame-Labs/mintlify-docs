Connect Gong so Endgame can learn from your team's calls. Endgame processes relevant call transcripts from Gong every hour and uses them in its insights and answers.

## Before you start

- You need to be an Endgame admin.
- The person connecting must be a [Gong Technical Admin](https://help.gong.io/hc/en-us/articles/360028568911-About-permission-profiles).

## Connect Gong

1. In [**Settings** > **Organization** > **Integrations**](https://app.endgame.io/settings/integrations), find Gong and click **Connect**. You can search for "Gong", or choose **Available** to show only integrations you haven't connected yet.
2. Sign in to Gong if it asks you to.
3. Click **Allow** to give Endgame access to your Gong account.

<Frame caption="Click Allow on Gong's authorization page to give Endgame access.">

You'll come back to the Gong page in Endgame, which shows **Connected** at the top.

It takes a day or two for Endgame to bring in your existing calls. After that, new transcripts are processed every hour.

<Info>
  Endgame uses Gong only to get call transcripts. It only sends transcripts to
  AI systems for accounts it has access to in Salesforce. If you haven't given
  Endgame access to an account, that account's Gong transcripts never touch an
  AI system.
</Info>

## Troubleshooting

If connecting Gong doesn't work, the person connecting is most likely missing the Technical Admin permission in Gong. A Gong admin can turn it on for them in Gong's [Team Members settings](https://app.gong.io/company/team-members).

## Update credentials or disconnect

Use the **Connection** section at the bottom of the Gong page. See [Manage a connected integration](/integrations/overview#manage-a-connected-integration).

- **Update credentials** takes you to Gong's sign-in page to renew Endgame's access.
- **Disconnect** stops syncing call transcripts from Gong and removes the call data Endgame already synced.
