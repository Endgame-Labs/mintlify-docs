Connect Highspot so Endgame can use the content in your Highspot Spots as knowledge. You choose which Spots to sync, and Endgame keeps them up to date.

## Before you start

- You need to be an Endgame admin and a Highspot administrator.
- You'll need three things from Highspot, found in **User Settings** > **Developer**: your **API URL**, a **client key**, and a **client secret**.

## Connect Highspot

1. In [**Settings** > **Organization** > **Integrations**](https://app.endgame.io/settings/integrations), find Highspot and click **Connect**.
2. Enter your **API URL**, **Client key**, and **Client secret**, then click **Connect**.

Endgame opens the Highspot page so you can choose what to sync.

## Choose Spots to sync

- **Add a Spot:** in **Available Spots**, click **Add** next to each Spot you want. Everything in a Spot you add syncs as knowledge every 15 minutes.
- **Pause a Spot:** in **Synced Spots**, click **Disable** to stop syncing new content from it. Click **Enable** to start again.
- **Remove a Spot:** in **Synced Spots**, click **Remove** to stop syncing it and stop using the content Endgame already synced from it.

## Update credentials or disconnect

Use the **Connection** section at the bottom of the Highspot page. See [Manage a connected integration](/integrations/overview#manage-a-connected-integration).

- **Update credentials** opens the same dialog as connecting, with nothing prefilled.
- **Disconnect** stops syncing content from Highspot. To reconnect later, you'll need your API credentials again.
