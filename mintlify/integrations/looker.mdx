Connect Looker so Endgame can use the results of the Looker queries you choose. Endgame runs your saved queries once a day and includes what it finds in Endgame chats.

## Before you start

- You need to be an Endgame admin and a Looker admin.
- You'll need three things from Looker: a **client ID**, a **client secret**, and your **tenant URL**. See [Get your Looker API credentials](#get-your-looker-api-credentials).

## Get your Looker API credentials

1. In Looker, click **Admin** in the left-hand menu.
2. Click **Users**, then click your own name.
3. Scroll down to **API Keys** and click **Edit Keys**.
4. Click **New API Key**.
5. Copy the **Client ID** and **Client Secret** and keep them somewhere safe. Looker only shows the client secret once.

Your **tenant URL** is the address you use to open Looker, such as `https://your-company.looker.com`.

## Connect Looker

1. In [**Settings** > **Organization** > **Integrations**](https://app.endgame.io/settings/integrations), find Looker and click **Connect**. You can search for "Looker", or choose **Available** to show only integrations you haven't connected yet.
2. Enter your **Client ID**, **Client secret**, and **Tenant URL**, then click **Connect**.

Endgame opens the Looker page so you can add the queries you want it to use.

## Add a query

1. In Looker, open the Explore for the data you want Endgame to use.
2. Click the gear icon at the top right, then click **Share**.
3. From the **Short URL**, copy everything after the last `/`. That's the query ID.
4. In Endgame, on the Looker page, click **Add query** next to **Saved queries**.
5. Paste the **Query ID**. Endgame shows a preview of the query's data so you can check it's the right one.
6. Add a **Title** and **Description**, then click **Add**.

A title and description help Endgame understand what the data means, and help you tell your queries apart later. Once you add a query, Endgame starts bringing in its data.

  Looker queries usually describe your whole organization, so Endgame treats
  them as organization-wide data rather than tying them to one account.
</Info>

## Delete a query

In **Saved queries**, hover over the query, click the trash icon, and then click **Delete**. Endgame stops using that query's data.

## Update credentials or disconnect

Use the **Connection** section at the bottom of the Looker page. See [Manage a connected integration](/integrations/overview#manage-a-connected-integration).

- **Update credentials** opens the same dialog as connecting, with nothing prefilled.
- **Disconnect** stops syncing your saved queries, and Endgame imports no new Looker data.
