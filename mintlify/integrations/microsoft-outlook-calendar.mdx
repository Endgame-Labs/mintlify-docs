title: "Microsoft Outlook Calendar"
description: "Connect to Microsoft Outlook Calendar to sync your calendar data"

---

<Warning>
  Access to Microsoft integrations is managed by your organization. If the
  Outlook integration isn't visible in Endgame, your organization hasn't enabled
  it yet.
</Warning>

Individual users can sync their Outlook calendars to provide more accurate and comprehensive meeting data for use in chat responses. This can be done in [Settings > Connections](https://app.endgame.io/settings/connections).

<Frame caption="Microsoft Outlook Calendar connection">
  <img
    src="../images/settings-connections-10-16-26.png"
    alt="Microsoft Outlook Calendar connection"
    className="rounded-lg"
  />
</Frame>

After clicking **Connect**, users will go through the Microsoft authentication flow to grant Endgame permission to ingest their calendar data.

<Frame caption="Microsoft account selection">
  <img
    src="../images/pick-microsoft-account-03-10-26.png"
    alt="Microsoft account selection"
    className="rounded-lg"
  />
</Frame>

Users can disconnect their Outlook calendar anytime by clicking **Disconnect** next to the Outlook Calendar integration. A confirmation modal will appear. Click **Disconnect** to confirm. Your calendar stops syncing and Endgame will no longer have access to future meeting data. Disconnecting doesn't delete meeting data Endgame has already synced.

    className="rounded-lg"
  />
</Frame>
