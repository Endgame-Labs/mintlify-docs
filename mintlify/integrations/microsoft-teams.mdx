Connect Microsoft Teams so Endgame can use your team's conversations about each account. You link Teams channels to accounts, and Endgame syncs their messages every 15 minutes. You can also sync Teams call transcripts.

## Before you start

- You need to be an Endgame admin and a Teams Global Administrator.
- You'll sign in with your Microsoft account and approve Endgame's access for your organization, so have that account ready.
- To sync call transcripts, a Microsoft Teams administrator will also need to run a PowerShell command once (see [Sync call transcripts](#sync-call-transcripts)).

## Connect Microsoft Teams

1. In [**Settings** > **Organization** > **Integrations**](https://app.endgame.io/settings/integrations), find Microsoft Teams and click **Connect**. Microsoft's sign-in page opens.
2. Pick the Microsoft account you want to connect.
3. On the permissions screen, check **Consent on behalf of your organization**, then click **Accept**.

## Link channels to accounts

**Chat channels** is turned on when you connect. To link a channel:

1. In **Add channel**, select a Teams channel. Channels are grouped by team.
2. Search for the account to link it to.
3. Click **Add**.

<Note>
  Endgame can't tell different accounts apart within a single Teams channel. Link channels that are dedicated to one account.

  Endgame _only_ syncs messages from the channels you link here. Other than the names of public channels, it doesn't access anything else in Teams.
</Note>

## Sync call transcripts

Endgame can also sync Teams calls and their transcripts. **Call transcripts** is turned on when you connect, but it needs a one-time setup in your Microsoft tenant first: a Teams administrator grants Endgame access through an Application Access Policy. Until that's done, the **Call transcripts** section shows **One-time tenant setup required**.

1. On the Microsoft Teams page, go to **Call transcripts** and copy the PowerShell command shown there.
2. Have a Microsoft Teams administrator run the command.
3. Back in Endgame, click **Verify access**. When it works, the page shows **Application Access Policy verified.**

{/* SCREENSHOT: new call-transcripts-setup-10-20-26.png. State image of the Call transcripts section before verification: the One-time tenant setup required message, the PowerShell command, and Verify access. */}

If verification fails, the page says why. Fix the issue, then click **Verify access** again. For help, contact [support@endgame.io](mailto:support@endgame.io).

## Update credentials or disconnect

Use the **Connection** section at the bottom of the Microsoft Teams page. See [Manage a connected integration](/integrations/overview#manage-a-connected-integration).

- **Update credentials** takes you to Microsoft's sign-in page, for example to connect with a different account.
- **Disconnect** stops syncing channel messages and calls from Teams. Endgame keeps your linked channels, so they sync again if you reconnect.
