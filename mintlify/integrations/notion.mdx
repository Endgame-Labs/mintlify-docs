Connect Notion so Endgame can use your team's Notion pages as knowledge. You choose the pages to sync, and Endgame syncs them along with every sub-page underneath.

## Before you start

- You need to be an Endgame admin and an admin of the Notion workspace you want to connect.
- Endgame connects to one Notion workspace at a time.
- You'll need an internal integration token from Notion. The next two sections walk through creating the integration in Notion and sharing your pages with it.

## Share pages with the integration

Notion only lets the integration read pages you share with it. Sharing a parent page also shares everything nested under it, so it's easiest to keep the content you want in Endgame under a few top-level pages.

You can share pages in either of two ways:

- **From a page:** open the page, click the **⋯** menu at the top right, then **Connections**, and choose your integration. Click **Confirm** to give it access.
- **From the integration:** on the integration's page in Notion, open the **Content access** tab, click **Edit access**, select the pages to share, and click **Save**.

## Connect Notion

1. In [**Settings** > **Organization** > **Integrations**](https://app.endgame.io/settings/integrations), find Notion and click **Connect**.
2. Paste the secret you copied from Notion into **Integration token**, then click **Connect**.

    src="/images/notion-connect-dialog-10-23-26.png"

Endgame opens the Notion page so you can choose what to sync.

## Choose pages to sync

Sharing pages in Notion gives Endgame access. You also tell Endgame which of those pages to sync, in **Page sync** on the Notion page in Endgame.

- **Add a page:** paste the page's URL into **Notion page URL** and click **Add page**. Share the page with your integration in Notion first. Endgame syncs the page and every sub-page under it, and lists it in **Synced page trees**.
- **Leave out some sub-pages:** after a page syncs, its sub-pages are listed below **Synced page trees**. Turn off **Sync** for any you don't want Endgame to sync. Content it already synced from them stays in Endgame. Turning off a page also turns off the pages under it. If the list isn't showing, or to pick up sub-pages added in Notion since the last sync, click the refresh icon next to the page.
- **Sync now:** Endgame syncs your pages every hour. To sync a page right away, click **Sync** next to it.
- **Remove a page:** click the trash icon next to a page, then **Remove**. The page and everything under it stop syncing, and the content Endgame synced from them is deleted. To stop syncing only some sub-pages, turn off **Sync** for them instead.

## Update credentials or disconnect

Use the **Connection** section at the bottom of the Notion page in Endgame. See [Manage a connected integration](/integrations/overview#manage-a-connected-integration).

- **Update credentials** opens the same dialog as connecting, with nothing prefilled. Paste a new token from Notion, for example after you refresh the integration's secret. Your synced pages stay in place.
- **Disconnect** stops syncing pages. Content Endgame already synced stays. To delete it, remove the page trees before you disconnect. To reconnect later, you'll need your integration token again.

You can also take away Endgame's access in Notion, for the whole integration or page by page.
