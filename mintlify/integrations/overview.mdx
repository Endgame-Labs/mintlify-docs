Integrations bring your team's calls, conversations, documents and CRM data into Endgame. Each integration you connect is shared by your whole organization, so only admins can connect, change or disconnect one.

This page covers what every integration has in common. Each integration also has its own page with its setup steps, under **Integrations** in the sidebar.

## Find your integrations

Your integrations are in [**Settings** > **Organization** > **Integrations**](https://app.endgame.io/settings/integrations). They're listed alphabetically, each with a short description of what it syncs.

- **Search** by name or by what an integration does, like "transcripts".
- **Filter** with **All**, **Connected**, or **Available** (not connected yet).

A check mark on an integration's logo means it's connected. A **Needs attention** badge means its connection has stopped working (see [Fix a connection that needs attention](#fix-a-connection-that-needs-attention)).

## Connect an integration

1. In **Settings** > **Organization** > **Integrations**, find the integration and click **Connect**.
2. Finish connecting. What happens next depends on the integration:
   - **Integrations you sign in to** (Gong, Microsoft SharePoint, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Zoom, and Confluence) take you to that tool's sign-in page. Sign in and approve access, and you'll come back to Endgame.
   - **Integrations that use credentials** (Chorus, Clari Copilot, Highspot, Looker, Notion, Seismic, and Snowflake) open a dialog. Enter the credentials from that tool and click **Connect**.
3. Endgame opens the integration's page, where you can finish any setup it needs, like choosing which spaces, sites, or channels to sync.

Salesforce is the exception: you normally connect it during onboarding, before you can use the rest of Endgame. It still appears in this list, so you can manage it later, unless your organization brings in Salesforce through Snowflake. See [Connect Salesforce during onboarding](/integrations/salesforce#connect-salesforce-during-onboarding).

Each integration's docs page lists what you'll need before you connect and walks through where to find it.

## Manage a connected integration

To open an integration's page, click **Manage** next to it. The page shows **Connected** at the top, the integration's own settings, and a **Connection** section at the bottom.

The **Connection** section shows:

- **When it was connected, and by whom.** If someone reconnected it later, that's shown too. Endgame only shows the person when it recorded who connected it.
- **Credentials:** click **Update credentials** to give Endgame new credentials, for example after you rotate a key or change the account Endgame connects with. For integrations you sign in to, this takes you to the tool's sign-in page. For the others, it opens the credentials dialog, for example **Update Notion credentials**. Nothing is prefilled, so have the new credentials ready.
- **Disconnect:** click **Disconnect** to stop syncing. Endgame asks you to confirm and tells you what stops syncing.

## Fix a connection that needs attention

For Chorus, Highspot, Microsoft Teams, Notion, Seismic and Snowflake, Endgame can tell when the credentials it uses stop working, for example because a password changed or a key expired. It stops syncing from that integration and flags it:

- On the Integrations page, it shows **Needs attention** and a red **Reconnect** button.
- On its own page, a message says it needs to be reconnected, and **Reconnect** appears at the top.

Click **Reconnect** on the Integrations page to open the integration's page, then click **Reconnect** at the top and sign in again or enter new credentials. While a connection needs attention, its **Connection** section doesn't show **Update credentials**. Syncing picks up again once it's reconnected.

Other integrations aren't flagged this way. If data stops arriving from one of them, open its page and click **Update credentials** to sign in again or enter new credentials.

If an integration shows **Status unavailable**, Endgame couldn't check its connection just then. Click **Open** to go to its page, or try again in a moment.
