Connect Salesforce so Endgame knows your accounts, opportunities, contacts and the people working them. Endgame syncs the standard fields on your core objects, plus any custom fields and extra objects you choose.

<Note>
  If your organization brings Salesforce data into Endgame through Snowflake, Salesforce doesn't appear on your Integrations page. See [Salesforce via Snowflake](/integrations/salesforce-via-snowflake) instead.
</Note>

## Before you start

- You need to be an Endgame admin.
- Decide which Salesforce user Endgame connects with. This is your **integration user**: Endgame syncs data with its credentials, so it only sees what that user can see in Salesforce. We recommend a [dedicated API integration user](#create-a-dedicated-api-integration-user) rather than a personal account.
- The integration user needs the `Approve Uninstalled Connected Apps` permission in Salesforce.
- Someone in Salesforce will need to [approve the Endgame connected app](#approve-the-endgame-connected-app) after you connect.

## Connect Salesforce during onboarding

Salesforce is the first thing you connect. It's part of onboarding, not something you set up from Settings like other integrations: until Salesforce is connected, signing in to Endgame opens a welcome screen that asks you to connect it.

1. On the welcome screen, under **Connect Salesforce**, choose where to connect:
   - **Production:** click **Connect**.
   - **Sandbox:** next to **Using a sandbox?**, click **Connect here**.
2. Sign in to Salesforce as your integration user and approve access.
3. In Salesforce, [approve the Endgame connected app](#approve-the-endgame-connected-app) so Endgame can start receiving data.

Endgame then starts bringing in your Salesforce data. This usually takes a day or two, depending on the size of your Salesforce instance, and the Endgame team will be in touch when your data is ready. For the full onboarding steps, see [Login & Connect CRM](/implementation-guide/login-and-connect-crm).

## Manage Salesforce in Settings

Once you're connected, Salesforce appears in [**Settings** > **Organization** > **Integrations**](https://app.endgame.io/settings/integrations). Click **Manage** next to it to open the Salesforce page. It works differently from other integrations:

- You don't connect Salesforce from here; that happens during onboarding.
- You can [choose important custom fields](#choose-important-custom-fields), [sync more objects](#sync-more-objects), and [change the integration user](#change-the-integration-user).
- Salesforce can't be disconnected from Settings. In the **Connection** section at the bottom of the page, next to **Disconnect Salesforce**, click **Contact support** to email [support@endgame.io](mailto:support@endgame.io).

If you connected a sandbox, the page is titled **Salesforce Sandbox**.

<Note>
  If your organization lets Endgame write changes back to Salesforce from chat, each person also connects their own Salesforce login in **Settings** > **You** > **Connections**.
</Note>

## Approve the Endgame connected app

<Warning>
  Approving a connected app in Salesforce requires the [Customize Application
  AND either the Modify All Data OR Manage Connected
  Apps](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=005132365&type=1)
  permissions.
</Warning>

Salesforce requires connected apps to be approved before Endgame can receive data. After you sign in to Salesforce from Endgame, in Salesforce go to **Setup** > **Connected Apps** > **Connected Apps OAuth Usage** and click **Install** next to the `EndgameLabs2` app.

## Fix an approval error when signing in

If you see an `OAUTH_APPROVAL_ERROR_GENERIC` error while signing in to Endgame, check these in Salesforce:

- **Connected app approval:** the `EndgameLabs2` connected app must be [approved](#approve-the-endgame-connected-app) before users can sign in.
- **Permitted users:** go to **Apps** > **Connected Apps** > **Manage Connected Apps** > **EndgameLabs2** and check **Permitted Users**. It has two options:
  - **All users may self-authorize:** everyone can access Endgame without pre-approval in Salesforce.
  - **Admin approved users are pre-authorized:** each person needs a profile or permission set that gives them access to the app.
- **Integration user:** the integration user needs the `Approve Uninstalled Connected Apps` permission to connect, and for the `EndgameLabs2` app to show up in Salesforce.

## Create a dedicated API integration user

This is optional, but recommended. Endgame reads data with the Bulk API 2.0, so you can connect with a dedicated API-only user. When you create the user in Salesforce, select:

- **User License:** Salesforce Integration
- **Profile:** Minimum Access - API Only Integrations
- **Additional permissions:** `Approve Uninstalled Connected Apps`

Then sign in as this user when you [connect Salesforce](#connect-salesforce-during-onboarding).

## How Endgame syncs your data

Endgame uses the Bulk API 2.0 to fetch data from Salesforce every hour, so the integration generally has little effect on your API limits. It syncs the required objects by default, and you can [add more objects](#sync-more-objects). After the first sync, Endgame only requests records that have changed since the last one (based on `SystemModStamp`).

Endgame checks for each query job to finish with an increasing backoff, starting at one minute and going up to ten minutes between checks. It downloads results in pages of 25,000 records, several pages at a time.

Endgame syncs data with the integration user's credentials. Only accounts that user can access in Salesforce are run through any AI systems, and everyone in your Endgame organization has access to the data synced for that user. To change the integration user, see [Change the integration user](#change-the-integration-user).

## Choose important custom fields

Endgame adds the standard fields on the required objects to its context graph. Custom fields are synced too, but Endgame doesn't reference all of them automatically: Salesforce instances often have hundreds of custom fields, and only a handful really help people understand their accounts. You choose which custom fields matter on Account, Contact, Opportunity and User.

1. In Endgame, on the Salesforce page, under **Required objects**, click **Account**, **Contact**, **Opportunity** or **User**. You can also hover over the row and click **Configure**.
2. In **Configure object fields**, search for the fields you want Endgame to use and select the checkbox next to each one. To have Endgame suggest fields, click **Recommend** and review what it selects.
3. Optionally, add a description to a field to explain what it means.
4. Click **Save**.

Field selections are reviewed by an Endgame representative. Saving files a request, and we'll reach out once the change has been made.

<Warning>
  You can select up to 100 fields per object. Standard fields, and custom fields that aren't populated on any records, aren't listed.
</Warning>

## Sync more objects

You can add more Salesforce objects to Endgame's context graph. At the top of the Salesforce page, Endgame shows how many objects you're syncing out of your limit, which counts every type of object together.

- **Standard objects:** under **Additional standard objects**, turn on the toggle next to each object you want. Turning a standard object on or off files a request that an Endgame representative reviews, and we'll reach out once the change has been made.
- **Custom objects:** under **Custom objects**, turn on the toggle next to each object you want. Custom objects sync automatically, and changes can take up to 2 hours.

## Change the integration user

1. In Endgame, at the bottom of the Salesforce page, in the **Connection** section, click **Update credentials**.
2. Sign in to Salesforce as the new integration user and approve access.

Endgame syncs with the new user from then on. Update credentials connects to the same kind of instance you chose during onboarding, production or sandbox.

To renew Endgame's access without changing the user, follow the same steps and sign in as your current integration user.
