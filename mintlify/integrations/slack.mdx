Connect Slack so Endgame can use your team's conversations about each account. You link Slack channels to accounts, and Endgame syncs their messages every 15 minutes. The channels you choose can also run Endgame threads right in Slack. See [Endgame in Slack](/features/slack).

## Before you start

- You need to be an Endgame admin.
- In Slack, you need to be a Slack admin or get approval from one. Check with your Slack admin before you connect.

## Connect Slack

1. In [**Settings** > **Organization** > **Integrations**](https://app.endgame.io/settings/integrations), find Slack and click **Connect**. Slack's sign-in page opens.
2. If you're not a Slack admin, you'll see a page to request approval. Submit the request, and Slackbot messages your Slack admins. Slackbot lets you know whether they approve it. If this page doesn't appear, you already have approval.
3. Click **Allow** to give Endgame the permissions it needs.

Endgame opens the Slack page so you can choose which channels to sync.

## Choose channels to sync

The Slack page has two kinds of channels:

- **Organization-wide channels** are for multi-account chats with Endgame, like `#sales-team`. Endgame syncs their messages, but doesn't use them to analyze individual accounts.
- **Account-specific channels** are linked to one account, like `#deal-acme`. Endgame links their messages to that account.

### Link channels to an account

1. In **Account-specific channels**, search for the account.
2. Select one or more channels for it, like `#deal-acme` and `#acme-help`.
3. Click **Add**.

Each account appears once in the list. To change an account's channels, open the ⋮ menu next to it and click **Edit channels**. To stop syncing them all, click **Remove all channels**.

### Add organization-wide channels

1. In **Organization-wide channels**, select the channels you want.
2. Click **Add**.

To change or remove one, open the ⋮ menu next to it and click **Edit channel** or **Remove channel**.

<Note>
  Endgame _only_ syncs the channels you add here. Other than the names of public channels, it doesn't access anything else in Slack.
</Note>

## Update credentials or disconnect

Use the **Connection** section at the bottom of the Slack page. See [Manage a connected integration](/integrations/overview#manage-a-connected-integration).

- **Update credentials** takes you to Slack's sign-in page to renew Endgame's access.
- Slack can't be disconnected from Settings yet. Click **Contact support** and we'll disconnect it for you.
