Connect Zoom so Endgame can learn from your team's meetings. Endgame brings in your Zoom meetings (past and upcoming), their participants, and their recordings.

Endgame uses them to show the meetings you've had on an account and which opportunities they relate to, and to build a fuller picture of your conversations with customers and contacts.

## Before you start

- You need to be an Endgame admin.
- The person connecting must be a Zoom administrator.

## Connect Zoom

1. In [**Settings** > **Organization** > **Integrations**](https://app.endgame.io/settings/integrations), find Zoom and click **Connect**. You can search for "Zoom", or choose **Available** to show only integrations you haven't connected yet.
2. Sign in to Zoom if it asks you to.
3. Review the permissions **Endgame for Zoom** asks for, then click **Allow**.

You'll come back to the Zoom page in Endgame, which shows **Connected** at the top. Endgame then starts bringing in your Zoom data automatically.

## Troubleshooting

If connecting Zoom doesn't work, check that the person connecting is a Zoom administrator.

## Update credentials or disconnect

Use the **Connection** section at the bottom of the Zoom page. See [Manage a connected integration](/integrations/overview#manage-a-connected-integration).

- **Update credentials** takes you to Zoom's sign-in page to renew Endgame's access.
- **Disconnect** stops syncing call transcripts and recordings from Zoom.

### Remove the app from Zoom

You can also remove the Endgame app from Zoom's side:

1. Sign in to the [Zoom App Marketplace](https://marketplace.zoom.us/).
2. Click **Manage** > **Added Apps**, or search for the **Endgame for Zoom** app.
3. Click **Remove**.

<Info>
  Once the app is removed, Endgame stops showing new meetings and transcripts
  from Zoom. Endgame doesn't automatically delete data it already synced. To
  ask for it to be removed, contact [support@endgame.io](mailto:support@endgame.io).
</Info>
