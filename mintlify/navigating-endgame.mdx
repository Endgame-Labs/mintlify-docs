## About Endgame

<Warning>
  Note: Endgame is moving to a new web app and MCP experience built on our
  context graph. These docs describe the new experience. Docs for the previous
  version live in [Legacy Endgame](/legacy-endgame).
</Warning>

Endgame enables everyone at your company to deeply understand your customers and prospects. Think of it as a single playbook for the entire team, covering sales and account management use cases such as company research, meeting prep, account planning, deal review, handoffs, and more.

After you [log in](https://login.endgame.io/), you'll land on the Endgame [homepage](https://app.endgame.io/), where you can ask Endgame about any entity in your organization's context graph.

## Search

Search is available from the input at the top of every page, or by pressing `⌘K` from anywhere in the app. Use it to dig into the entities in your context graph, such as accounts, people, meetings, and channels.

Results are grouped by category as you type, and each result carries a short label to help you tell similar entries apart. Before you type anything, Endgame lists what you visited most recently. Click any result to open its entity page and see everything Endgame has assembled on it.

## Entity pages

Each entity page brings together everything the context graph knows about that entity. Alongside cited factual data, you'll find compiled insights and rollups produced by Endgame's context agents, which run around the clock to keep every entity current. Sections powered by a context agent are labeled with the name of the agent providing the data.

## Chat

Use chat to ask questions about a specific account or across your entire book, just like you would with any AI assistant. Dig into meetings, people, deal details, and more. To open chat, click the text bubble icon in the top navigation.

#### Chat history

Your past threads are listed in the **Chat history** column on the left, most recent first, with the age of each thread next to its title. Endgame titles threads automatically based on what you asked, so you can scan the list to pick up earlier work. Click any thread to reopen it and keep going, or select **View all chats** at the bottom of the column to browse your full history. **Hide chats** collapses the column to give the conversation more room, and **+ New** in the top right starts a fresh thread.

#### Chat controls

The controls above a thread let you save it, share it, or remove it.

<Frame caption="Chat controls">
  <img
    src="./images/chat-nav-08-28-26.png"
    alt="Chat header showing the copy link icon, New button, and the star and overflow menu next to the thread title"
    className="rounded-lg"
  />
</Frame>

- **Favorite:** Click the star icon next to the thread title to favorite a chat. Favorited chats are pinned to the top of your **Chat history** so you can get back to the threads you return to most.
- **Copy chat link:** Click the link icon in the top right to copy a link to the chat. Share it with anyone in your organization to give them the full thread, including your questions and Endgame's responses and citations. Links only work for people in your organization who are logged in to Endgame.
- **More options:** Click the **...** menu next to the thread title to delete the chat. Deleting removes the thread from your chat history and can't be undone.

#### Adding files to a chat

You can attach files to any chat by clicking the **+** button in the chat input. Once added, files appear above the input. To remove a file before submitting, click the **X**.

JSON, XML, HTML, Markdown, plain text (.txt, .log), and images (.png, .jpg,
.gif, .webp). The maximum file size is 5 MB per file._

#### Access skills in chat

Your organization skills are accessible in chat. Click on **+** to open the menu and select or browse skills to use in your chat. You can also type `/` to view available skills.

## Quick navigation tips

### Link to accounts in Endgame

You can link directly to a specific account in Endgame using the account's Salesforce ID. Some organizations find this particularly helpful for linking from Salesforce records into Endgame.

Use the following URL format, replacing `[SALESFORCE ACCOUNT ID]` with the account's Salesforce ID:

`https://app.endgame.io/accounts/[SALESFORCE ACCOUNT ID]`

Example: `https://app.endgame.io/accounts/0011234567890ABC`
