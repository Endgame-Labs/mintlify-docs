## About Endgame

<Warning>
  Note: Endgame is moving to a new web app and MCP experience built on our
  context graph. These docs describe the new experience. Docs for the previous
  version live in [Legacy Endgame](/legacy-endgame).
</Warning>

Endgame enables everyone at your company to deeply understand your customers and prospects. Think of it as a single playbook for the entire team, covering sales and account management use cases such as company research, meeting prep, account planning, deal review, handoffs, and more.

After you [log in](https://login.endgame.io/), you'll land on the Endgame [homepage](https://app.endgame.io/), where you can ask Endgame about any entity in your organization's context graph. Search sits at the top of the page, use it to dig into accounts, people, channels (where applicable), and recent meetings. Click any result to open its entity page and see everything Endgame has assembled on it.

## Entity pages

Each entity page brings together everything the context graph knows about that entity. Alongside cited factual data, you'll find compiled insights and rollups produced by Endgame's context agents, which run around the clock to keep every entity current. Sections powered by a context agent are labeled with the name of the agent providing the data.

## Chat

Use chat to ask questions about a specific account or across your entire book, just like you would with any AI assistant. Dig into meetings, people, deal details, and more. To open chat, click the text bubble icon in the top navigation.

#### Adding files to a chat

You can attach files to any chat by clicking the **+** button in the chat input. Once added, files appear above the input. To remove a file before submitting, click the **X**.

_Supported file types: PDF, Word (.doc, .docx), Excel (.xls, .xlsx), CSV, TSV,
JSON, XML, HTML, Markdown, plain text (.txt, .log), and images (.png, .jpg,
.gif, .webp). The maximum file size is 5 MB per file._

#### Access skills in chat

Your organization skills are accessible in chat. Click on **+** to open the menu and select or browse skills to use in your chat. You can also type `/` to view available skills.

## Quick navigation tips

### Link to accounts in Endgame

You can link directly to a specific account in Endgame using the account's Salesforce ID. Some organizations find this particularly helpful for linking from Salesforce records into Endgame.

Use the following URL format, replacing `[SALESFORCE ACCOUNT ID]` with the account's Salesforce ID:

`https://app.endgame.io/accounts/[SALESFORCE ACCOUNT ID]`

Example: `https://app.endgame.io/accounts/0011234567890ABC`
